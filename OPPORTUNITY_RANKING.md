## Selected experiment

Create a private offline demo of **select → propose → review → export** using synthetic media. Test with 3–5 consenting users.

Success criteria:

- at least 3 users finish without help;
- at least 2 say they would repeat weekly or pay;
- zero personal photos, network calls or automatic publishing;
- errors and task time are recorded anonymously.

A failed result is useful: stop or redesign rather than continue by sunk cost.
